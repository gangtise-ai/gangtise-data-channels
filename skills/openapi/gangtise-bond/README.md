# Bond Data

Pull structured data on bonds and issuers. Refer to a bond by short name, pinyin, or code.

---

## When to use

- Look up bond terms, quotes, yield to maturity, and ChinaBond / Shanghai Clearing House valuations
- Map coupon and principal cash flows, and exercise schedules for bonds with embedded options
- Track issue and issuer ratings and their history for credit work
- Check rate-bond issuance plans, bond announcements, and the issuer's business-registration profile

## How to use

- "Closing price and yield to maturity for 23国债15 over the past month"
- "Rating-change history and exercise schedule for H1万科04"
- "Treasury and local-government bond issuance plans for the next 30 days"
- "Look up this issuer's rating and outstanding bonds"

## Output

CSV tables with Chinese column names, plus a result preview.
