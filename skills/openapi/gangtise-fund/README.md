# Mutual Fund Data

Pull mutual-fund profiles, holdings, AUM, holder structure, NAVs, and ETF creation and redemption data.

---

## When to use

- Screen and diligence a fund: basics, fees, and manager history
- Analyze holdings: asset allocation, industry mix, and top stock, bond, and fund positions
- Track changes in AUM and holder structure, including the top ten holders
- Follow ETF creation and redemption baskets and daily share changes

## How to use

- "Past managers and top holdings of E Fund Blue Chip Select"
- "How this fund's industry allocation has changed over recent periods"
- "CSI 300 ETF share count and AUM over the past week"
- "Zhang Kun's years in the industry and assets under management"

## Output

CSV tables with Chinese column names, plus a result preview.
