# Research Agent Text

Pull Gangtise research write-ups in one place: one-pagers, investment theses, peer comparisons, earnings reviews, viewpoint debates, theme tracking, research outlines, hot topics, and leads.

---

## When to use

- Get up to speed on a company: key facts, investment thesis, and competitive landscape
- Review earnings-season commentary, or argue both sides of a market view
- Scan the day's hot topics and core names before the morning meeting
- Pull recent research leads on a stock or industry from reports, transcripts, and filings

## How to use

- "Give me the one-pager and investment thesis for Kweichow Moutai"
- "Latest earnings review for CATL"
- "Viewpoint debate: the solar capacity clear-out is nearly over"
- "Hot topics and core names in this morning's brief"

> The text is updated on a schedule and can lag. Pair it with live data and document search.

## Output

Markdown research text you can quote directly. Hot topics and leads come back as structured tables.
