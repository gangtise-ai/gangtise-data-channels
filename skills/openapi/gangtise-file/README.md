# Research Document Search

Find documents in the Gangtise file center by type, date, security, and other filters, and download the full text.

---

## When to use

- Find recent research reports, filings, and meeting notes on a stock
- Look up foreign research, chief-analyst views, official-account articles, and investor Q&A
- Browse the research calendar: roadshows, company visits, strategy conferences, and earnings dates
- Search charts inside research reports for illustrations

## How to use

- "Broker research on SMIC from the past month"
- "Kweichow Moutai's 2025 annual report and the management discussion from the earnings call"
- "Which pharma companies have roadshows or visits next week"
- "Find a few research charts on energy-storage installations"

> Use Knowledge Base Search when you need to read and quote passages directly.

## Output

A document list (title, date, institution, abstract, file ID). Full text can be downloaded as PDF or Markdown.
